Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5): the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week, at most 20 words.

The program writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`. The line: **"Power outage took out my whole street, electrician says it won't be back until tonight. Sorry boss."** — plausible, hurried, and impossible to disprove from the office.

## How it works

`main.ts` trims the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, nothing else.
